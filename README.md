# CzAEasy
After Effects ScriptUI Panel plugin to help with Crazy Error content creation

# How to install
1. Download CzAEasy.jsx from the repo
2. Move it to "C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe After Effects 20XX\Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels" (or wherever you installed After Effects on your device)
3. Open After Effects
4. In the top bar menu, click on the Window menu, then at the bottom of it you should see CzAEasy.jsx appear. Just click on it to reveal the panel into your workspace.
